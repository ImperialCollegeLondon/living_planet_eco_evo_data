Analysis of acoustic data in R

This section of the module introduces techniques for analysing ecological acoustic data using R.

The practicals include:

  • Loading and visualising ecological acoustic data as spectrograms.

  • Calculating ecological acoustic indices from acoustic data and comparing values through time and between sites.

Practical data¶

The practicals use two sets of acoustic data recorded as a series of WAV format files. Both sets of recordings were made at dawn in two locations:

  • Monks Wood - a woodland National Nature Reserve in Cambridgeshire.

  • Parsonage Down - is this correct?
